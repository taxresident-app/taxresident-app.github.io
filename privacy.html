TAX Resident

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

TAX Resident keeps your data on your iPhone and, if you turn on iCloud Sync, in your own iCloud account. We have no servers, no analytics and no advertising, and we never sell your information. The only personal data we receive is what you choose to send us by e-mail.

1. Who we are

TAX Resident ("the App") is developed and published by the TAX Resident team, which is responsible for your data under this policy (the "controller" under the GDPR). You can reach us at taxresident365@gmail.com.

2. Data stored in the App

Everything you enter or the App records is stored in a database on your device:

  • Profile — the name and nationality you enter
  • Day records — the country (and, in the United States, the state) and the name of the city or town for each day, entered by you or recorded automatically, plus your planned trips
  • Documents — passport, visa and residence-permit details and expiry dates, and any photos of them you add
  • Settings — language, notifications, app lock and similar preferences

This data does not leave your device unless you turn on iCloud Sync or export it yourself (sections 4 and 5).

3. Location

Location access is optional. You can record every day by hand instead.

If you allow it, the App uses Apple's location services to work out which country you are in — when you check in, and, if you choose "Always" and turn on automatic detection, in the background when iOS reports that you have arrived somewhere new. The App does not track you continuously.

To turn a position into a country, the App uses Apple's built-in geocoding service, which sends the position to Apple and is covered by Apple's privacy policy. The App then keeps only the country (and US state), the name of the city or town, and the date. It never stores or sends your coordinates, and we never receive any location data.

You can change or revoke location access at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → TAX Resident.

4. iCloud Sync

iCloud Sync is off until you turn it on in the App's settings. When it is on, the App keeps an encrypted copy of your data in the App's private container in your own iCloud account, so that it stays the same on all your Apple devices. Apple stores this data under its own privacy policy; we have no access to it.

Document photos are not synced — they stay only on the device where you added them.

5. Backups you export

When you use Export or Backup, the App creates a file and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes next is your choice. From version 1.10 you can protect the file with a password (it is then encrypted with AES-256). Without a password the file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe.

6. Camera, photos and Face ID

The App asks for camera or photo-library access only when you add a photo of a document. Photos are stored inside the App on your device.

If you turn on the app lock, iOS checks your Face ID or Touch ID. The App never sees or stores your biometric data.

7. Notifications

Reminders (for example, a daily check-in or a document about to expire) are scheduled on your device. No notification content passes through any server of ours.

8. When you contact us

If you write to us — from the App's "Send Feedback" button or directly — we receive your e-mail address, your message and any device details included with it (app version, iOS version, device model, language, region, and the result of the App's built-in encryption self-test). None of your day records or documents are included. We use this only to answer you and to improve the App, keep it in our support mailbox, and delete it on request. Our mailbox is provided by Google (Gmail).

9. Crash reports

If you have chosen in iPhone Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us anonymous crash and usage reports. We use them only to fix problems. You can switch this off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

If the App itself runs into an error, it may offer to open a pre-filled e-mail to us with the error details, app version and iOS version. Nothing is sent unless you send that e-mail yourself.

10. What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-up
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking tools
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data
  • No selling or sharing of your data
  • No in-app purchases — the App is free

11. Keeping and deleting your data

Because your data is on your device and in your iCloud account, you control it. To delete it:

  • delete the App from your device;
  • if you used iCloud Sync, also remove the App's data in iPhone Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage;
  • delete any backup files you exported.

To have your e-mails with us deleted, write to us.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, UK or California), you have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of personal data we hold about you, and to object to its use. For data in the App and in your iCloud, you can do this yourself. For e-mails you have sent us, write to us and we will reply within one month. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

13. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly receive data from them.

14. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version here with a new effective date. If the App ever starts collecting data, we will ask for your consent in the App first.
