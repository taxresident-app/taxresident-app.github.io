Frequently Asked Questions

How does day counting work?

TAX Resident counts each day you check into a country toward that country's tax residency threshold. Most countries use a 183-day rule within a calendar or fiscal year. You can check in manually or enable automatic location detection.

Is my data private?

Yes. All your data stays on your device and in your personal iCloud account. We don't have servers that receive your information. No analytics, no tracking, no ads. Read our full Privacy Policy.

Can I use the app without location services?

Absolutely. Location services are optional. You can manually check into any country at any time using the check-in feature on the home screen.

How do I export my tax day report?

Go to the Tax tab, then tap the export/share icon. The app generates a PDF report with your complete location history and day counts per country, ready to share with your tax advisor.

What does Premium include?

The free app includes full tracking, day counting, document storage, and visa management. Premium adds country-specific tax intelligence: detailed residency rules, common exceptions, treaty information, and links to official tax authority guidance.

Does TAX Resident provide tax advice?

No. TAX Resident is a personal tracking and reference tool. It does not provide legal, tax, or immigration advice. Always consult a qualified tax advisor or immigration attorney for your specific situation.

How do I restore my data on a new device?

If you have iCloud enabled, your data syncs automatically. Simply install TAX Resident on your new device, sign in with the same Apple ID, and your data will appear.
